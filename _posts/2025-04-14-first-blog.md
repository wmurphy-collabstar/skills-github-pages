Here is my first blog. I've never made a blog before, so I'm open to all the mistakes I make in the process of starting this journey.
Growing up as a perfectionist, I felt like life was 3x as hard, and pursuing my goals nearly impossible. But the thing I never questioned, 
was my impossibly-high standards. I thought that I had to be perfect all the time, and even as I write this, I worry about posting writing that isn't the best.
But I'm not perfect, and perfection is *impossible* to achieve. So cheers to my first steps of falling on my face and being proud of myself
for doing so!
